## How to verify

Open [ARK Tribe HQ](https://www.arktribehq.com/), go to **Taming Lab**, search the creature, and verify **the entire animal is visible**, silhouette/limb proportions and species-defining features are recognizable, the outline color follows your theme, mobile cropping is correct, and no blank or reused artwork appears. For passive/specialty tames, continue into the appropriate planner. Mark the box only after you personally approve the appearance.

## Spawn map integration — v0.8.33

Every Taming Lab, Passive Taming Lab and Specialty Taming Lab now contains the **Creature Spawn Locations** atlas after the planner. Its creature and map are synchronized to the user's selection and the embedded Wiki page reloads when either changes. An external wiki map may require a second selection in its proprietary filter if it does not honor the supplied query parameter: no unverified spawn markers are drawn in this app.

## Adjacent-art bleed repair

The earlier 18 creature studies from two 3×3 sheets have been re-extracted by finding the actual connected individual silhouette across the source sheet, rather than clipping each rectangular cell. Every repaired illustration now uses a self-contained SVG with extra whitespace and excludes strokes belonging to a neighboring creature. **Visual acceptance on desktop/mobile is still required.**

## Technical safeguards

- Preserve approved Rex, original nine, and v0.8.28 predators
- Do not invent torpor, food or knockout quantities when wiki data are unavailable
- Do not count procedurally modified body-family outlines or unfinished traced snippets as final custom art
- Do not mark an illustration complete just because a gzip/base64 payload exists; a human still needs to inspect it at desktop and mobile widths
- Retain the searchable Taming Lab and compact knockout panel
