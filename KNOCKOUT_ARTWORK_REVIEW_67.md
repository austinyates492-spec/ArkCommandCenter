## How to verify

Open [ARK Tribe HQ](https://www.arktribehq.com/), go to **Taming Lab**, search the creature, and verify **the entire animal is visible**, silhouette/limb proportions and species-defining features are recognizable, the outline color follows your theme, mobile cropping is correct, and no blank or reused artwork appears. For passive/specialty tames, continue into the appropriate planner. Mark the box only after you personally approve the appearance.
